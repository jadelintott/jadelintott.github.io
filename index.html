About

Who I am and what I bring to the classroom

In the classroom, I focus on helping students build confidence while learning to ask sharper questions, make stronger arguments, and engage generously with one another. This site is designed to give hiring committees a clear, welcoming overview of my teaching profile.

On this site, hiring committees can quickly find teaching materials, research summaries, and a readable version of my CV. Students and collaborators can also use it to learn more about my courses and interests.

Teaching priorities

  • Inclusive discussion and transparent assignment design
  • Clear feedback that helps students revise with purpose
  • Courses that connect theory, practice, and public relevance
