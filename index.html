About

Who I am and what I bring to the classroom

In the classroom, I focus on helping students build confidence with abstract material by pairing rigorous expectations with accessible explanation, active problem solving, and responsive support. Much of my teaching has centered on helping students navigate mathematical foundations for computer science.

My recent work includes serving as lead instructor for Intro to Discrete Math for Computer Scientists at Georgia Tech, teaching binary linear algebra and graph theory in an inquiry-based summer program, and supporting students in algorithms and introductory computer science courses.

Teaching priorities

  • Make formal mathematical ideas approachable without lowering rigor
  • Use interactive problem solving, office hours, and discussion to build confidence
  • Support accessibility and multiple ways of learning technical material
