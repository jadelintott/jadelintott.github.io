Philosophy

Teaching statement snapshot

My teaching centers on making mathematically rigorous material approachable without reducing its depth. Across lectures, office hours, grading sessions, and inquiry-based classrooms, I aim to help students move between concrete examples, formal reasoning, and collaborative problem solving.

I am especially drawn to courses in discrete mathematics and theoretical computer science, where students benefit from clear exposition, repeated practice, and space to ask questions as they build confidence with proof, abstraction, and algorithmic thinking.

Pedagogical commitments

  • Explain technical and visual material accessibly for different learners
  • Use active problem solving and responsive office-hour support
  • Pair rigor with clear structure, examples, and iterative feedback
